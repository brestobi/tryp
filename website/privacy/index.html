Information we may handle

Depending on how a person uses TRYP, the service may need to handle account and profile information, contact details, driver and vehicle information, identity and onboarding documents, pickup and destination information, trip status, payment-related records, device information, and messages sent to support.

How information is used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, provide and coordinate rides, support scheduled bookings, verify driver onboarding, process payments, communicate important service updates, investigate support issues, improve reliability, and meet applicable legal or safety obligations.

Sharing and service providers

TRYP may use carefully selected infrastructure and service providers to operate authentication, data storage, maps, notifications, payments, analytics, and customer support. The final policy should identify the relevant categories and the safeguards that apply.

Location information

Location data is central to a ride service. The final policy must explain when location is collected, how it is used during a trip, how long it is retained, and how a person can manage device permissions.

Retention and rights

The final policy should specify retention periods and explain the rights available to people under the laws applicable to TRYP and its users, including how to request access, correction, deletion, or other available actions.

Questions about privacy

For privacy questions, contact hello@tryp.co.za until the official privacy address is confirmed.
